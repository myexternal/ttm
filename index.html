About Tiny Tot

At Tiny Tot, children choose their work, learn with their hands and grow at their own pace, with teachers who know them by name.

A first school that feels like a second home

Big shady trees, a yard full of swings and tunnels, and classrooms painted in every colour. Children learn the Montessori way: real materials, small tables, and the freedom to try again until they get it.

  • Well trained teachers
  • Sinhala and English letters
  • Outdoor play every day
  • Concerts, camps and festivals
